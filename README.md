# nellaijohnmuthu.com — build and operating notes

Static site. No build step, no database, no monthly cost beyond the domain.
Tamil is the primary language throughout; English appears as parallel summaries
so that journalists, party organisers and researchers who search in English also
land here.

```
index.html       Home — slogan, banner photo, who you are, record, centre
panchayats.html  35 local bodies, 467 issues, searchable. Tap a village to open it.
about.html       Biography, qualifications, the four European governance models
issues.html      Constituency-wide issues: law / ground reality / what was filed
work.html        The public ledger — every petition with date, office and status
help.html        Free petition guide. This is the page that brings strangers in.
assets/style.css
assets/img/      banner.jpg  banner-sm.jpg  office.jpg  og.jpg
robots.txt  sitemap.xml  CNAME
```

## 2. Buying the domain

Any registrar works. Namecheap, Cloudflare Registrar (cheapest, no upselling),
or an Indian registrar if you prefer paying in rupees.

Register **both**:
- `nellaijohnmuthu.com`
- `nellaijohnmuthu.in`

Point the `.in` at the same site. It costs a few hundred rupees and stops
someone from registering it later during a contested period and putting
whatever they like on it. Turn on WHOIS privacy and auto-renew. Set a calendar
reminder for the renewal date anyway — expired domains during an election
season are a known and very cheap attack.

## 3. Deploying

You already run npvk2031.github.io, so GitHub Pages will be familiar.

1. Create a **new** repository — keep this separate from the NPVK repo.
2. Upload every file in this folder, keeping `assets/` as a folder.
3. Settings → Pages → Source: `main` branch, root folder.
4. Settings → Pages → Custom domain: `nellaijohnmuthu.com`. The `CNAME` file
   is already in place.
5. Tick **Enforce HTTPS** once the certificate is issued (takes up to an hour).

At your registrar, set DNS as GitHub's current documentation specifies for an
apex domain — four `A` records and four `AAAA` records pointing at GitHub Pages,
plus a `CNAME` record for `www` pointing at `<your-username>.github.io`. Copy the
IP addresses from GitHub's own docs page rather than from any tutorial; they have
changed before.

Cloudflare Pages is a good alternative and gives you faster loading in India.

## 4. Search setup, in this order

1. **Google Search Console** — add the property, verify by DNS, submit
   `https://nellaijohnmuthu.com/sitemap.xml`. Then use "URL Inspection" and
   request indexing for each of the five pages individually. This is the
   fastest route to appearing in results.
2. **Bing Webmaster Tools** — takes five minutes, imports from Search Console.
3. **Google Business Profile** for the service centre. Category: something like
   "Social services organisation". This is what puts you on Google Maps when
   someone in Cheranmahadevi searches for petition help. For a local presence
   this is worth more than everything else in this section combined.
4. Put the domain in your **YouTube channel description and video descriptions**.
   Your channel is your only existing source of links, and links are what
   Google uses to decide a new domain is real.
5. Put it in your **WhatsApp status, printed material, the flex board at the
   centre, and the footer of every petition you hand over**. A printed URL on a
   petition receipt is a better long-term investment than any keyword.

## 5. What this site will and will not rank for

Be realistic about this so you do not waste money on people promising otherwise.

**It will rank quickly (weeks), because nothing competes:**
- "நெல்லை ஜான் முத்து", "Nellai John Muthu"
- "நெல்லை ஜான் முத்து மக்கள் சேவை மையம்"
- "Melapudukudi petition centre"

This alone achieves your first goal. When a journalist, a party organiser or a
curious voter searches your name, this is what they find, and it is a record of
work rather than a poster.

**panchayats.html is now your strongest page.** 467 issue lines naming real
villages, streets, tanks, tender IDs and rupee amounts is exactly the kind of
content nothing else on the Tamil web has. Someone in Pathamadai searching their
own village plus "வடிகால்" has almost no chance of finding anything today. That
page is why the site will get traffic from people who have never heard your name.

**It can rank in three to six months with steady posting:**
- "மனு எழுதுவது எப்படி", "மாவட்ட ஆட்சியர் மனு மாதிரி"
- "நத்தம் பட்டா பிரிவினை"
- "கிராமச் சபை கூட்டம் விதிகள்"
- village-name plus issue searches from within the constituency

This is where `help.html` earns its place. These are real searches by real people
in Tirunelveli, and almost nobody has written good Tamil answers to them. That
is your opening.

**It will not rank, and do not try:**
- "Ambasamudram MLA", "அம்பாசமுத்திரம் எம்எல்ஏ" — news sites and Wikipedia own
  these, and chasing them would make the site look like a campaign page, which
  is exactly the impression to avoid right now.

**Keep the two pages honest about their difference.** `panchayats.html` is
research — things you have documented. `work.html` is your record — things you
have filed. Never let a research line drift into the ledger. The moment you file
a petition on any issue in the directory, move it across with its date and
receipt. That migration, repeated, is your whole case for 2031.

**The one thing that decides all of this** is whether you add to `work.html`.
A site with five ledger entries in 2026 and fifty in 2029 is an unanswerable
argument. A site with five entries in both years is worse than no site, because
it documents that you stopped. Aim for one new ledger entry a week. Ten minutes.

## 6. About the panchayat directory

Built from `Chapter 8` — 35 local bodies, 467 issue lines, grouped into eight
categories (water, roads, drainage, agriculture, lighting, schools and health,
environment and safety, governance and land).

**Every ward-councillor table was excluded, as you asked.** Those tables carried
names, ages, caste, home addresses and personal mobile numbers. Publishing that
would have been a privacy problem and a legal risk, and it would have turned a
public-service resource into something that reads as opposition research. I also
stripped every phone number that appeared in the running text, including
panchayat secretaries. Keep that data in your private files where it belongs —
it is genuinely useful for your own escalation work.

The page needs no server: search and filtering run in the browser, and it works
with JavaScript disabled (everything is in the HTML, it just won't filter).

To add a village later, copy any `<details class="lb">` block and edit it.

## 8. Editing later

Everything is plain HTML. To add a ledger entry, copy an existing `<article
class="entry">` block in `work.html`, change the three fields, and put it at the
top of the list. Status options are: `entry-status` (red, pending),
`entry-status done` (green, completed), `entry-status open` (amber, ongoing).

If the site grows past about fifteen pages, split it into `/ta/` and `/en/`
sections with `hreflang` tags rather than keeping both languages on one page.
That is a good problem to have later, not a decision to make now.
